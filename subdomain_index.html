    Beginners Chess Program

    ✔ Foundations ✔ Special Rules ✔ Basic Tactics ✔ Game Analysis

    Our Beginners Program starts from the absolute basics. It is designed to introduce the beautiful game of chess to young minds, establishing strong logical thinking, deep analysis, and tactical fundamentals.

    🌟 Beginners Level 1 — Chessboard & Piece Fundamentals (Duration: 2-3 Months)
    • Board Foundations: Number of squares, names of squares, files, ranks, diagonals, and notations.
    • Piece Movements: Move mechanics, capturing hanging pieces, and values of Pawns, Knights, Bishops, Rooks, Queens, and Kings.
    • Defense Mechanisms: Good exchange vs. bad exchange, defense by moving away, capturing, supporting, or blocking.
    • Check & Escape: Creating a check, getting out of check (move, block, capture), checkmate vs check.
    • Basic Checkmates: Double Rook checkmate, basic puzzle solving, and standard chess notation.
    🌟 Beginners Level 2 — Special Rules & Core Tactics (Duration: 5-6 Months)
    • Special Moves: Castling, Pawn Promotion vs. Underpromotion, and En Passant.
    • Opening Fundamentals: Control of the center, development of pieces, and King safety.
    • Core Tactics: Checkmate in 1 move, double attack, Knight fork, pins, and skewers.
    • Discovered Attacks: Back rank checkmates, discovered checks, discovered attacks, and removing/destroying the defender.
    • Draw Rules & Clock: Stalemate, draw by agreement, threefold repetition, insufficient material, and basic chess clock usage.
    • Elementary Endgames: Checkmate with Queen & King, checkmate with Rook & King, and tactical puzzles.
    🌟 Beginners Level 3 — Practical Play & Etiquette (Duration: 3 Months)
    • Game Analysis: Practice games and post-mortem correction of piece applications.
    • Tactical Drills: Applying forks, pins, skewers, discovered checks, and mate patterns in real games.
    • Threat Detection: Identifying tactical opportunities and finding opponent's threats.
    • Mistake Correction: Basic opening errors, rules of thumb, and correction of practical moves.
    • Tournament Rules: Tournament etiquette, offline clock settings, and preparation for Intermediate syllabus.
    Calculate • Prep • Compete

    Intermediate Chess Program

    ✔ Tactical Training ✔ Opening Preparation ✔ Tournament Prep

    Designed for players who know intermediate rules and have basic tactical vision. This module develops calculation speed, tactical patterns, standard openings, and mid-game strategy for tournament play.

    🌟 Intermediate Level 1 — Tactical Vision & Major Openings (Duration: 6 Months)
    • Tactical Drills: Mate in 2 moves, Mate in 3 moves, and Double Attack (Level 2).
    • Tactics Upgrades: Knight fork (Level 2), Pins (Level 2), Back Rank (Level 2), and Skewers (Level 2).
    • Discovered Actions: Discovered check & attack (Level 2), and destroying the defender (Level 1 & 2).
    • Opening Preparation: Deep analysis of Queen's Gambit (Accepted & Declined), Sicilian Defence (including Anti-Sicilians), Caro-Kann Defence, and French Defence.
    🌟 Intermediate Level 2 — Advanced Calculation & Repertoire (Duration: 6 Months)
    • Calculations training: Mate in 4 moves, and Double Attack (Level 3).
    • Tactics Elite: Knight fork (Level 3), Pins (Level 3), Back Rank checkmate (Level 3), and Skewers (Level 3).
    • Discovered Mastery: Discovered check & attack (Level 3), and destroying the defender (Level 3).
    • Opening Repertoire: Detailed theory on King's Indian Defence, Modern Defence, Pirc Defence, and Ruy Lopez.

    Advanced Chess Program

    Our Advanced Program is tailored for serious tournament players. It focuses on strategic concepts, precise calculation, grandmaster level openings, master-level tactics, and technical endgames to prepare students for FIDE tournaments.

    🌟 Level 1 — Advanced Strategic & Tactical Concepts (Duration: 6 Months)
    • Positional Calculation: Calculation training, positional understanding, and active practical play.
    • Tactical Concepts: Overloaded pieces, Line Opening, and Line Closing methods.
    • Strategic Maneuvers: Square Vacation, Passed Pawns control, and the Zwischenzug (in-between move).
    • Attacking Combinations: Drawing combinations, X-Ray Attacks, and Windmills.
    🌟 Level 2 — Advanced Tactics & Attacking Checkmates (Duration: 6 Months)
    • Attacking Calculations: Advanced tactics, checkmating patterns, and elementary checkmate with Bishop & Knight.
    • High-Level Combinations: Introduction to chess tactics, and the art of combining pieces.
    • Strategic Offense: Tactical decoy and deflection drills.
    🌟 Level 3 — Endgame Fundamentals & FIDE Prep (Duration: 6 Months)
    • Endgame Calculations: The Concept of Opposition, and the Rule of the Square.
    • Piece Endings: King & Pawn Endings, Queen vs Pawn, Knight vs Pawn, and Rook vs Pawn.
    • Elite Endgames: Queen vs Rook, and fundamentals of Rook Endings.
    • Tournament Prep: Guidance for official FIDE Rated Tournaments, building tournament confidence, and practical play.

    Children can start learning chess from age 5 onwards. At this age, their brain begins absorbing spatial coordinates and movement parameters rapidly, laying a strong foundation for mathematical reasoning.
