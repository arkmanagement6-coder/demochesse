    Foundation & Basics Curriculum

    ✔ Beginner Friendly ✔ Structured Curriculum ✔ Puzzle Training ✔ Tournament Basics

    This course starts from absolute scratch. Ideal for kids who are fresh to chess or know minimal board layout rules. We introduce interactive gamified puzzles to build immediate logical speed.

    🌟 Beginners Level 1 — Chessboard Foundations (Duration: 2 Months)
    • Chessboard basics: Files, ranks, diagonals, coordinates, and coordinates.
    • Piece movements & values: Detailed moves of Pawns, Knights, Bishops, Rooks, Queens, Kings.
    • Concept of Check & Escape: Move, Block, Capture rules.
    • Checkmate patterns & Draw: Scholar's mate, stalemate, 50-move draws.
    • Basic Chess Notation: Writing down game moves (Kxd5, e4, etc.).
    🌟 Beginners Level 2 — Basic Tactics & Captures (Duration: 2 Months)
    • Introduction to Castling, Promotion, and En Passant.
    • Primary Tactical Weapons: Forks, Skewers, Pins.
    • Piece Trades: Safe captures vs. bad trades.
    • Solving beginner mate-in-1 and mate-in-2 tactical puzzles.
    🌟 Beginners Level 3 — Endgame Basics (Duration: 2 Months)
    • King and Queen vs. Lone King Checkmate mechanics.
    • Double Rook & Single Rook Checkmate patterns.
    • Introduction to Pawn Promotion endgames.
    • Basic Chess Etiquettes & Tournament clock settings.
    Rating: 800 - 1200 Elo

    Tactics & Strategy Syllabus

    ✔ Tactical Calculation ✔ Opening Principles ✔ Tournament Preparation

    Designed for students who are fluent with chess board rules and basic mate-in-1 concepts. This premium module covers tactical planning, chess openings theory, defense strategies, and middle-game spatial structures.

    Frequently Asked Questions

    Find instant answers to questions regarding trial formats and tournament guidance.

    What is the right age to start chess?
    Children can start learning chess from age 5 onwards. At this age, their brain begins absorbing spatial coordinates and movement parameters rapidly, laying a strong foundation for mathematical reasoning.
    Are classes online or offline?
    We provide both online live classes (globally accessible via interactive whiteboard portals) and structured physical offline classes at our premium training hubs.
    Do you prepare students for tournaments?
    Yes, absolutely! We provide complete pre-tournament opening preparation, speed-clock psychology, and direct FIDE rating guidelines to get kids competition-ready.
    How many classes per week?
    We usually schedule 2 live interactive sessions per week depending on the student's program intensity, paired with weekly mandatory match practice schedules.
    Do beginners need prior experience?
    No prior experience is required at all. Our Beginners Program starts from the absolute fundamentals of the chessboard, pieces values, and checkmate patterns in a highly engaging, gamified format.
